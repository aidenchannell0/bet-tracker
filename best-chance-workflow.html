Best Chance — build workflow

Anchor deep, then climb one line at a time until the odds land in the window. Deterministic — you can trace any build by hand.

flowchart TD
  START(["Best Chance build
target = e.g. $2.00"]):::start --> P1
  P1["①  PICK THE LINEUP
Rank every player by cushion headroom
take the top ~5–6 (more legs for higher targets)
so their deepest lines land just under target"]:::step --> A2
  A2["②  ANCHOR DEEP
set every player to his shortest line = max cushion
multiply the legs → combined odds"]:::step --> D1

  D1{"Combined odds
vs target?"}:::dec
  D1 -->|within ±20¢| FIN(["✓ FINISH
this is your multi"]):::finish
  D1 -. "over (low target)" .-> DROP["Drop a leg
fewer legs = lower odds"]:::guard
  DROP -. retry .-> A2
  D1 -->|under| D2

  D2{"Any player still
below his Solid ceiling?"}:::dec
  D2 -->|yes| B4["③  BUMP +1 LINE
climb the highest-headroom player one line (still Solid)
at his Solid ceiling (2 below worst-of-5) → next player"]:::step
  B4 -- re-check --> D1
  D2 -->|no · all maxed| D3

  D3{"Any players left
on the bench?"}:::dec
  D3 -->|yes| S5["④  SWAP A LEG
drop the lowest-odds leg
bring in a fresh bench player (pays more, still Solid)"]:::step
  S5 -- retry --> A2
  D3 -->|no| STOP["⑤  STOP — safest build
return the safest multi for these games
flag: add a game or lower the target"]:::stop

Finish — odds landed in the window Bump loop — ③ → re-check (climb lines) Swap loop — ④ → retry (change players) Stop — pool exhausted, flag it
Two rules that keep it safe:
· Every leg always sits at Solid+ cushion (≥ 2 below the player's worst of his last 5). The bump never crosses that ceiling.
· It climbs the safest player first (most headroom) and only one line at a time, so it stops the instant it's in the window — never over-bumps.
