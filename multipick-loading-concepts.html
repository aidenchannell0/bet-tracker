MultiPick. loading concepts

Concept A

Neural pulse

Player props as nodes; the model's "thinking" lights up paths between them. Lime accents pulse along randomly-firing connections, suggesting the search exploring combinations. Subtle, sophisticated — feels like AI reasoning, not a slot machine.
